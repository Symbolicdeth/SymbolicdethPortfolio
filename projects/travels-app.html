ANDROID / TRAVEL PLANNING

TravelsApp

A bilingual Android travel planner for organizing trips, itineraries, bookings, notes and travel documents on-device.

Project Overview

TravelsApp is a Kotlin Android app for keeping trip details together. It supports Spanish and English, stores travel information locally, and includes itinerary, booking and document workflows.

Key Features

  • Create and edit trips with destinations and dates
  • Build itineraries with map links and completed activities
  • Keep notes, reservations and travel documents per trip
  • Switch between Spanish and English

Technology Stack

Kotlin
Jetpack Compose
Android
